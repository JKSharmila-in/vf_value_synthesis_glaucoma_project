Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Where is my feedback stored?

A: Feedback is stored locally on your machine. No data is transmitted externally. You can review the records folder in the project repository.

Q: Can I submit feedback without providing my name or email?

A: Yes, name and email are optional. However, providing an email allows us to follow up with you if needed.

Q: What should I include in a bug report?

A: Please describe the exact steps to reproduce the issue, what you expected to happen, and what actually happened. Include your browser and operating system if relevant.

Q: How is clinical feedback used?

A: Clinical observations from ophthalmologists are invaluable for validating model predictions and identifying areas for improvement in the training process.

Q: Can I request new features?

A: Absolutely! Select "Feature Request" as the feedback type and describe the feature you'd like to see. We review all suggestions carefully.
